JET'S PIZZA

Known for its signature Detroit-style deep-dish pizza, Jet's Pizza offers online ordering for pickup and delivery.

Overview Problem & Solution Research & Persona Solutions Overview Conclusion Next Steps

01 / Overview

Guest shoppers ordering pizza online often face uncertainty from unclear item pricing, misleading combo graphics, and missing pickup details that destroy trust during checkout.

Driven by user persona research and clear interface strategy, this project redesigned the ordering flow and receipt experience to explicitly display dish inclusions as $0 Included, keep pickup locations visible, and deliver a reliable order confirmation, building total customer confidence.

02 / Problem & Solution

PROBLEM / 01

Misleading choices

Breadsticks appear in the photo but aren't included. Vague labels hide what happens next.

SOLUTION / 01

Clear choices & actions

State what's included and label every next step.

PROBLEM / 02

Confusing numbers / unclear price method

Included toppings and drinks appear with misleading positive cost modifiers (+$1.98, +$1.30) during builder selection.

SOLUTION / 02

Transparent price structure

Explicitly display included items as $0 / Included. Use "+$" price tags exclusively for paid optional extras.

PROBLEM / 03

Missing order details

The street address disappears during checkout. The receipt has no order lookup number.

SOLUTION / 03

Details that stay visible

Keep the full street address visible throughout checkout. Add order number and customer details.

PROBLEM / 04

Unbranded, unclear email receipt

The receipt customer receives via email lacks brand identity such as logo, branch address, order number, customer's name, and clear pricing or items included in the order.

SOLUTION / 04

Complete branded receipt

Add brand's logo, branch address, order number, customer's name, and clarify pricing and items included in the order.

03 / Research & Persona

Research & Usability Audit

I ran a test session watching a guest shopper order on the live website while carefully mapping out every step of the checkout flow. Combining real-time user feedback and a targeted usability survey with my own detailed review of the screens helped me uncover severe friction points: overall satisfaction dropped to 28%, price clarity scored 1 / 5 stars, and location confidence hit 0% due to missing order and address details.

Pain points & Goals

PAIN POINTS
  • Store location address drops off during checkout, showing only city and state.
  • Misleading combo graphics display items that are not actually included in the price.
  • Included options display positive add-on pricing (+ $1.98) instead of $0.
  • Confirmation email lacks order reference numbers and full store details for pickup.
GOALS & SOLUTIONS
  • Keep full branch street address visible across all checkout screens.
  • Align menu graphics strictly with included combo items to prevent confusion.
  • Display transparent $0 pricing for baseline included choices.
  • Include prominent logo header, full store address, and order lookup numbers on receipts.

02 / Show what the combo includes

Problem

The promo graphic visually displays breadsticks that are not part of the combo deal. "Choice of Bread" confuses sides with crust.

Goal & Solution

Separate crust options from side items. Mark pizza and drink as included. Label breadsticks as extra.

03 / Make every price clear

Problem

Included items show extra charge indicators (+$1.30) creating confusion during item customization.

Goal & Solution

Show $0 for included items; charge only for extras. Label changes and back navigation clearly. Use an explicit continue button.

04 / Build trust after checkout

Problem

The original receipt email lacks branding, a readable order number, or full customer pickup context.

Goal & Solution

Add branding and a prominent order number. Include customer, pickup address, and order time. Itemize the total and state "Pay at Store".
