02 / Mobile game UX

Livin’ America Onboarding usability audit

Livin America, by Global Origin, is a free, choice-driven U.S. life simulation from age 18 to retirement, blending decisions, mini-games, adult skills, and milestones with optional purchases.

Overview Problems and Solutions Methodology User Persona Pain-points and Goals Strategic Recommendations

01 / Overview

My walkthrough found engaging gameplay that teaches real-life skills, with opportunities to reach more age groups, simplify language, improve animations, and make choices clearer.

02 / Problems and Solutions

Item 1 of 4

Age Fit

Problem

Appeals mostly to 18-year-olds starting out.

Solution

Expand story tracks for adults in their 30s–40s going through career transitions.

Language Barriers

Problem

Slang and complex jargon trip up international players.

Solution

Use plain language and clear context for non-native English speakers.

Gameplay Variety

Problem

Practical life choices can feel repetitive after early stages.

Solution

Introduce interactive mini-games for interview practice, stock trading, and employment law.

UI & Pacing Issues

Problem

Fast auto-scrolling and cut-off login buttons disrupt player flow.

Solution

Let players control text speed, smooth out animations, and fix layout spacing.

03 / Methodology

Research Validation & Data Sources

  • Primary Method: 80-minute walkthrough, heuristic evaluation, and cognitive mapping.
  • Secondary Data: Sentiment analysis of 300+ iOS App Store reviews (4.7 ★ avg).
  • Demographic Grounding: Validated via 13+ App Store age rating and player milestone feedback.

User Voice - non-native English Speaker

"I was curious why the app decided that I am 18 years old, especially since I registered as being older than that. Moving to another country, specifically the USA, is part of the lives of people older than 18 who have higher education and work experience."

1. Legal Disclaimer Screen

Step Analysis & Recommendation: "The disclaimer needs to be simpler to understand." Dense legal copy at initial launch creates onboarding friction.

Optimization Recommendation

Show main legal points in short bullet points, with a link to read full terms if needed.

2. Login Options & Button Layout

Step Analysis & Recommendation: "The lower button is cut off. Add username/password option." Layout elements clipping on certain screen dimensions affect visual polish.

Optimization Recommendation

Fix bottom spacing so buttons aren't cut off, and support standard login input fields.

Step Captured

3. Google Login Transition Animation

Step Analysis & Recommendation: "The photo animation moved in a funky way and was slow." Transition timing following authentication shapes performance impression.

Optimization Recommendation

Speed up loading animations and make them fade in smoothly without progress freezes.

Step Captured

4. Graduation Prompt Framing

Step Analysis & Recommendation: "Who are you... truly? Direction needed." Structural indicators orient players when encountering reflective story choices.

Optimization Recommendation

Add a clear label like [Scenario Question] above the prompt and frame choices in first-person statements.

Step Captured

5. Trait Selection Guidance & Mechanics

Step Analysis & Recommendation: "Can I select more than one trait?" Clear visual UI affordances establish single vs. multi-select expectations.

Optimization Recommendation

Use standard radio buttons and add a short helper note explaining to pick only one trait.

6. Microcopy & Global Accessibility ('Hazard Pay')

Step Analysis & Recommendation: "Love the humor! But non-native speakers might miss 'hazard pay'." Witty personality enriches brand while accessibility ensures global comprehension.

Optimization Recommendation

Maintain the fun tone while ensuring core game rules are explained clearly for all audiences.

7. Family Wealth Text Logic

Step Analysis & Recommendation: "Sentences contradict with 'But' when they shouldn't." Conjunctions work best when highlighting explicit contrast.

Optimization Recommendation

Rewrite text logic: "Everyone's story starts at the beginning... But not everyone starts with equal resources."

8. Spinning Wheel Progress & Context

Step Analysis & Recommendation: "Waited 30 seconds and screen got stuck on 'Not bad...'." Progress freezes hurt trust; context prior to spinning sets expectation.

15. Creative Career Paths & Information Cards

Player Observation: "I chose to work in Design, and there were only 2 options: architect and interior designer. I felt like I would like a representation of graphic designers. Usability Context: The flip-card design pattern effectively informs player choices; expanding career options enhances creative representation.

Optimization Recommendation

Optimization Idea: Keep flip card design, but add more creative job paths like Graphic Designer and UX/UI Designer.

17. Demographic Representation Across Life Stages

Step Analysis & Recommendation: "Relocating to the USA happens across all age groups and experience levels." Multiple life-stage branches expand player market reach.

Optimization Recommendation

Offer optional setup tracks (Student, Young Professional, Career Relocator) during onboarding.

Strategic Recommendations

Key recommendations to expand product reach and optimize usability.

1. Expanding Demographic Reach

Introduce story tracks tailored for adult career pivoters alongside core student tracks.

2. Localization & Plain Language

Provide simplified descriptions to ensure global non-native English speakers navigate logistics smoothly.

3. Simulation Feature Extensions

Integrate practical mini-games for job interviews, financial trading, and employment law basics.
